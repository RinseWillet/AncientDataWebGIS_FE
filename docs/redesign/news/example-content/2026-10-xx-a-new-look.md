AncientData has a new design. A calm sage “Day” style and a night-blue “Night” mode; a flat basemap that leaves the landscape to the elevation models; roads drawn by how certain they are; and records that say, in words, what is attested and what is inferred. On the phone, the map now gets the whole screen.

[And for the curious: press F9.]
